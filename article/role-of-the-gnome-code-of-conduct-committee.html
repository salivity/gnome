Role of the GNOME Code of Conduct Committee

The GNOME Code of Conduct Committee is responsible for establishing, monitoring, and enforcing behavioral standards across the GNOME project to ensure a welcoming, inclusive, and professional environment. This article outlines the committee’s primary duties, including receiving and investigating incident reports, mediating conflicts, applying appropriate sanctions, and fostering psychological safety across GNOME’s communication channels, repositories, and community events.

Defining and Communicating Behavioral Expectations

The committee works to interpret and uphold the GNOME Code of Conduct across all project spaces, including GitLab repositories, mailing lists, Matrix and IRC channels, and in-person events such as GUADEC. By setting clear boundaries regarding acceptable and unacceptable behavior, the committee establishes a shared baseline of mutual respect, constructive criticism, and collaboration for both new and long-standing contributors.

Processing Reports and Conducting Inquiries

When a community member experiences or witnesses harassment, discriminatory language, or other violations, they can submit an incident report directly to the committee. The committee handles these reports through a structured process:

  • Confidentiality: Protecting the privacy and safety of reporting parties and witnesses to prevent retaliation.
  • Fact-Finding: Gathering context, message logs, and relevant evidence to evaluate the situation objectively.
  • Impartiality: Recusing committee members with conflicts of interest to ensure fair assessments for all parties involved.

Enforcing Sanctions and Conflict Resolution

The primary objective of the committee is restoring a safe environment rather than simply punishing contributors. Depending on the severity and frequency of the violation, the committee determines proportional responses, which may include:

  • Private educational warnings outlining the problematic behavior.
  • Facilitated apologies or restorative mediation between parties.
  • Temporary mutes or suspensions from specific communication platforms.
  • Permanent bans from GNOME infrastructure, conferences, and foundation activities in severe cases.

Advising Governance and Promoting Transparency

Beyond handling individual incidents, the committee acts as an advisory body to the GNOME Foundation Board of Directors. It recommends updates to existing policies, tracks recurring behavioral trends within the community, and publishes periodic transparency summaries that inform the public about the types of issues addressed without compromising individual confidentiality.

Sustaining Community Health

By maintaining accountability and clear channels for redress, the GNOME Code of Conduct Committee helps lower barriers to entry for underrepresented groups in open source. Their ongoing work ensures that the GNOME ecosystem remains productive, cooperative, and aligned with its core mission of delivering accessible, free software for everyone.
